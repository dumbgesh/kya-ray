# Kya-Ray

*A little context-aware AI desktop companion.*

Kya-Ray helps you understand and work with whatever you're looking at, right where you are.

Select something. Press **`Ctrl + Alt + Space`**. Keep going.

![Kya-Ray — product vision](assets/kya-ray-vision.png)

The goal is simple: Kya-Ray understands what you need without making you choose a mode. It automatically infers the right task from your selection:

- **Explain** — Unpack unfamiliar words, technical concepts, and terminology.
- **Summarize** — Condense longer passages, articles, and meeting notes.
- **Code** — Explain code snippets and terminal commands, diagnosing genuine syntax issues with zero invented errors.
- **Create** — Draft quick replies, messages, rewrites, and creative text.

Built around local AI via Ollama (`llama3.2:1b`), with 100% offline privacy and staying out of your way at the center.
